## Outcome
Ran the three NGE extras jobs end to end in their committed form on a full provincial fwapg (a 128 GB machine with its own local Docker database, same FWA load as the #11 build), checked SSNbler on the groups and whole basins that had been too large, and cut upstream branches. The main-flow tree reproduced #11 exactly. The PCIC crosswalk's first full run exposed a nondeterminism: about 1% of outlets sit on a vertex two segments of one blue line share, and with no tie-break the spatial index's scan order chose the segment. The user chose the segment starting at the vertex (FWA's `[drm, urm)` convention); 74 crosswalk rows now differ from #11, all ties. The topology QA's BC-boundary test was rebuilt to union BC once (35 -> 1.5 min, identical rows). A serial SSNbler driver with a memory watchdog (`ssnbler_check.sh`) was written, reviewed and rebuilt so its exit status has one owner. SSNbler turned out to have two very different paths: serial below 46,340 lines (memory quadratic) and parallel above it (about linear), and the parallel path's unsnapped-node test is chunk-local, so it is not reliable. Durable findings are in `research/fwa_mainflow_tree.md` and `research/fwa_position_codes.md`. Follow-up: fwapg#14 (flow and placement at a tributary junction's vertex). Upstream PRs are drafted in `upstream_pr_*.md`, with branches `upstream-blue-line-paths`, `upstream-pcic-crosswalk` and `upstream-mainflow-tree` (the latter two stacked on the first) on the fork. Nothing has been posted upstream.

- PCIC: run 1 (no tie-break) 2 rows off #11; run 2 (lowest id) 449 off; run 3 (upstream segment) 74 off, all ties (same measure within 1 mm). The one segment that lost flow is the Toba's 75-122 m, below its terminal outlet.
- SSNbler 1.1.2, peak footprint: serial USKE 21,655 lines 28 GB (4 errors, the known 1.6 cm spot), MSKE 24,732 37 GB (4, the 2.9 cm spot), LSKE 29,266 52 GB (0), BULK 30,046 52 GB (0), FRAN 30,134 54 GB (0).
